What stands out

Highlands is the cheapest traffic

Escape to the Highlands delivered 886 visits at 5.5p each, 31% of all landing page views on a quarter of the budget. Its CPM (£6.00) is the lowest of the four, so the US audience is reaching it cheaply.

SO Escape to the Edge costs most

At 8.9p per visit it is 62% dearer than Highlands, driven by a £10.28 CPM rather than weaker ads. CTR is in line with the rest. West Coast of Ireland sits close behind at 7.9p.

One ad carries each campaign

In Highlands and NC500 a single image took over 97% of spend, so the other creatives in those campaigns have barely been tested. Ireland and SO spread spend more evenly across two or three images.

Source: Meta Ads Manager, ad account Absolute Escapes, pulled 13 Oct 2026 for the last 7 complete days (6–12 Oct), default attribution. Spend in GBP. All other campaigns in the account, including the UK SWCP and WHW tests, were paused and had no delivery. The 14% link CTR is far above what Meta traffic campaigns usually see (around 1–2%); it is worth checking placement mix for accidental clicks, although the 81% click-to-landing-page rate suggests most clicks are real visits.
