// The wrong budget fight

Channel CPA tells you where the sale was recorded.

Blended CAC tells you what it cost to create the customer.

META · ATTRIBUTED CPA

£10,000 spend

50 attributed customers

£200

GOOGLE · ATTRIBUTED CPA

£10,000 spend

100 attributed customers

£100

THE OBVIOUS CALL

Move the money from Meta to Google.

If cutting Meta means fewer people search for you later, that £100 CPA was never independent of it.

The question isn’t which channel has the cheapest CPA. It’s what combination gives the lowest profitable CAC at the volume you want.
