// Evidence · Binet & Field, the IPA Databank

This is not a new argument. It is just newly measurable.

Les Binet and Peter Field analysed 996 campaigns entered into the IPA Effectiveness Awards between 1980 and 2010 and found an optimum balance of roughly 60% demand creation to 40% demand capture. The channels changed. The physics did not.

60%

Brand building · demand creation

Works slowly, compounds, builds the memory that makes tomorrow’s search happen at all.

40%

Sales activation · demand capture

Works fast, does not compound, converts the demand that already exists.

This is not a prescription to spend 60% on Meta and 40% on Google. It is evidence for a broader principle: demand creation and demand capture have to be funded together.
