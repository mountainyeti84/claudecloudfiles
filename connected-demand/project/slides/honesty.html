// How to read this evidence

A relationship is a strong signal. It is not a shortcut to causality.

Correlation tells you whether two measures moved together over time. It does not isolate creative changes, offer shifts, seasonality, pricing, competitor activity or sales follow-up. Any of those can be the real cause.

That does not make it useless. When a pattern repeats across 55 accounts, matches independent holdout testing, and fits how people actually describe buying, it earns the right to be treated as a serious hypothesis and tested properly.

Use this evidence to ask sharper questions. Do not use it to claim automatic attribution.
