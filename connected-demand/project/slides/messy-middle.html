// Independent research · Google

Buyers do not move down a funnel. They loop.

Google’s Decoding Decisions research calls the space between a trigger and a purchase the messy middle: two mental modes people cycle through, as many times as they need, across feeds, search, reviews and aggregators.

Exploration widens the set of options. Evaluation narrows it down. A brand that is absent during the loop never enters the set being narrowed.
