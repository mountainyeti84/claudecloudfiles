// A journey the report cannot see

Fourteen days. Five touchpoints. One line of credit.

DAY 0

Instagram

A Reel stops the scroll. No click, no visit, no conversion. Nothing in the report.

DAY 3

Google

They search the brand name. Not the category — the brand. That memory came from somewhere.

DAY 6

Research

Reviews, a blog, a comparison, a video. Confidence is quietly being assembled.

DAY 11

Direct

They return on their own. Still not ready. Still not credited anywhere.

DAY 14

Brand search

They search, click the ad and buy. Google Ads books 100% of the revenue.

Everything before day fourteen is invisible to last-click reporting. Remove the first step and the last one gets harder — slowly, and with no obvious cause.
