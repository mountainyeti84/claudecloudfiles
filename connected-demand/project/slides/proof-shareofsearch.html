Search volume is not just an outcome. It is a forecast.

by which share of search movements tend to precede share of market — up to a full year in categories such as automotive.

Les Binet analysed automotive, energy and mobile handsets and found that a brand’s share of category search correlates with market share — and moves first.

“Fast, cheap and predictive.”

LES BINET · EFFWORKS GLOBAL 2020

Which reframes the whole debate. If demand shows up in search before it shows up in revenue, then anything that lifts branded search is not a cost centre. It is a leading indicator of next year’s growth.
