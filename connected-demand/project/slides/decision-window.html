// Setting the measurement window

Set the window from your own purchase lag.

A £40 ecommerce product and a £5,000 holiday should not inherit the same measurement window. The shape below is one common progression, not a universal law.

WEEK 1–4

New audiences enter

Prospecting expands. People arrive who have never heard of you.

WEEK 5–8

Efficiency dips

Search activity rises before conversions do. The report looks worse.

WEEK 8–10

They research

Comparing, revisiting, reading reviews, building confidence.

WEEK 10–12

Impact appears

Conversion rates climb and PPC ROAS recovers.

THE DECISION RULE

Set the window before launch from your own lead-to-sale data. Never let short-term platform efficiency alone kill activity designed to influence future demand.
