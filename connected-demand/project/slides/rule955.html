Most of your market is not shopping today.

Professor John Dawes of the Ehrenberg-Bass Institute put a number on it: at any given moment roughly 5% of potential buyers are in market. The other 95% are not searching, not comparing and not reachable through intent.

Search can only sell to the five. The job of everything else is mental availability — being the brand the other ninety-five think of first when their moment arrives.
