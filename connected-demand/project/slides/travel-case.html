03 / A WORKED EXAMPLE

THE ADS YETI

// Where the connected journey is impossible to ignore

A trek is not an impulse purchase.

Someone sees a destination story. Thinks about their fitness. Asks a friend. Compares itineraries. Speaks to an expert. Then books — often months later, through a brand search. The gap is so long that last-click reporting stops being merely incomplete and becomes actively misleading.

Inspiration

Make the outcome and the identity shift feel real.

Reassurance

Answer fitness, safety, training and payment early.

Proof

Let the community make the risk feel survivable.

Continuity

Search and sales resolve the questions creative raised.

Travel is the clearest illustration, but the same shape applies to any considered purchase: B2B software, finance, furniture, education.
