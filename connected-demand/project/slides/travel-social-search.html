// A high-consideration example: Travel

Social owns the dreaming. Search owns the deciding.

STAGE ONE · INSPIRATION

77%

of travellers use social media during the inspiration phase of a trip

69%

expect to use social to plan travel in the next year — rising to 87% of Gen Z and 81% of Millennials

STAGE TWO · DECISION

61%

use a search engine in the 45 days before booking, and 80% visit an OTA

19%

say advertising influenced their booking — which is what every travel brand’s attribution is arguing over

The stage that creates the wish and the stage that captures the booking are different channels. Tour operators say the same: ATTA’s 2026 survey of 329 operators found word of mouth and social media the most effective marketing channels, while the majority of bookings still land direct.
