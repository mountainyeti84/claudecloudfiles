Meta ROAS dips

02

Budget moves to Search

Sensible on paper. Search is efficient, measurable and closest to revenue.

03

Branded search softens

Four to eight weeks later. Nobody connects it to the decision in step two.

04

Acquisition costs rise

Less new demand entering the market. The same spend chases a smaller pool.

05

It looks like PPC broke

It did not. There were simply fewer new customers entering the journey.

Every decision looked sensible when each channel was viewed alone. The mistake was treating them as if they were alone.
