Don’t use a channel metric to answer a business question.

Layer The question it answers What you actually look at
01 Channel efficiency Did the channel do what we asked? Spend, reach, frequency, creative signal, audience quality.
02 Demand response Did the market react? Branded-search trend, qualified traffic, direct visits, PPC conversion rate.
03 Commercial outcome Did the business get better? New customers, blended CAC, blended MER, contribution after marketing.

Keep measuring Meta and Google individually. Just stop making the budget decision from those two numbers alone.
